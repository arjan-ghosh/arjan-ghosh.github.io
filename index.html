01About

Arjan Ghosh completed his M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Khulna University, Bangladesh, and holds a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BRAC University in Dhaka. He currently works as a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Northern University of Business and Technology in Khulna, Bangladesh.

His research spans Natural Language Processing, advanced Artificial Intelligence, the Internet of Things, communications and machine learning, and he serves as a reviewer for a number of international conferences. He values his past teaching roles for the mentoring experience they gave him and for what they taught him about working closely with students.
